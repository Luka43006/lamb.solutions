# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Growing service businesses and SMBs that want to eliminate repetitive work, reduce operational costs, and scale using AI automation. They hire Lamb Solutions to streamline workflows, automate manual processes, and build systems that save time and increase efficiency.

**Primary market: Serbia / the Serbian-speaking region.** The site is written and served in Serbian (not localized/bilingual — Serbian is the primary and only language for now).

## Product Purpose

Lamb Solutions designs and builds AI agents, automations, and custom software that remove manual busywork from a client's operations. Success means fewer hours lost to repetitive processes, lower operational cost, and systems that scale as the client grows.

## Positioning

Undecided. Lamb Solutions is pre-revenue and still defining what makes it different from other AI automation agencies and no-code shops. Do not assert a specific competitive claim (e.g. "engineering depth" vs. "no-code," "full custom software" vs. "workflow tools") until the user confirms one.

## Operating Context

The current site presents four service disciplines — Workflow Automation, AI Agents, Custom Software, and Systems Integration — plus a fifth area, Data & Analytics Pipelines. The engagement model follows a four-stage process: Discover, Design, Build, Operate. The primary conversion path is a contact/inquiry form ("Book a Call").

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Confirmed services: workflow automation (approvals, reconciliation, reporting), AI agents (support/triage/research handled end to end), custom internal software, systems integration, data & analytics pipelines/dashboards.
- Company stage: pre-revenue, still defining positioning. Copy and future work should not present Lamb Solutions as an established agency with a proven track record.
- Undecided / open: specific target industries, pricing and engagement model, team size and structure beyond "still defining."

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Lamb Solutions.
- Contact email: info@lamb.solutions.
- Voice: confident, editorial, unhurried — reflected in the existing site copy (e.g. "no deck required," "we'll tell you honestly whether automation is the right move").

## Evidence on Hand

None yet. The current case study ("order-to-cash... mid-market logistics operator," 90-person ops team), the testimonial ("COO, Mid-Market Logistics Operator"), and the trust-strip stats (40+ workflows automated, 12 industries served, 94% average error reduction, 3-week average time to deploy, 31+ hours saved per client weekly) are confirmed placeholder/illustrative content, not real client results. Future work must not treat these as fact, cite them as proof, or extend them with further invented specifics — flag them for replacement once real evidence exists.

## Product Principles

1. Automate the manual and repetitive first, not the strategic or judgment-heavy work.
2. Build durable systems with monitoring and audit trails, not fragile one-off scripts.
3. Design around the client's actual process — the tool adapts to the operation, not the reverse.
4. Scope honestly — tell a prospect plainly when automation isn't the right move before selling anything.
